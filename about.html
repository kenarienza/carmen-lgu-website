Our Story

History & Geography

The Municipality of Carmen is a fourth-class agricultural municipality located in the province of Surigao del Sur, within the Caraga Region (Region XIII) of Mindanao, Philippines. Surrounded by fertile farmland and home to the scenic Carmen Natural Spring, Carmen's economy is anchored on agriculture, tourism, and small-scale trade.

The municipal center sits at approximately 9°14' North, 126°1' East, at an elevation of roughly 13 meters above sea level. Carmen is composed of 8 barangays, with the Poblacion serving as the seat of local government and commerce.

Since the 1970s, Carmen has grown steadily — from just over 5,600 residents in 1975 to nearly 11,720 residents as of the 2020 Census, with an annual population growth rate of about 2.66%.

Local Economy

What Drives Carmen

Carmen's economy is rooted in the industries that sustain its agricultural communities and growing tourism sector.

Tourism

Home to the Carmen Natural Spring, a popular getaway for local and visiting tourists.

Agriculture

Rice, coconut, and root crops remain the backbone of Carmen's farming barangays.

Local Trade & MSMEs

The Poblacion hosts the municipality's public market and growing small enterprises.
