8 Barangays

Our Barangay Communities

The Municipality of Carmen is composed of 8 barangays, each led by an elected Barangay Captain and Sangguniang Barangay.

1

Antao

A farming barangay contributing to Carmen's agricultural output.

2

Cancavan

Home to ongoing farm-to-market road infrastructure projects.

3

Carmen (Poblacion)

The municipality's seat of government, commerce, and public market — the largest barangay by population.

4

Esperanza

A community known for local health and outreach programs.

5

Hinapoyan

A riverside barangay supporting local farming livelihoods.

6

Puyat

An agricultural community within the municipality's interior.

7

San Vicente

A growing residential and farming barangay.

8

Santa Cruz

Home to the Carmen Natural Spring, the municipality's popular tourist attraction.

Local Governance

Working With Your Barangay

Each barangay in Carmen operates its own Barangay Hall where residents can request clearances, certificates of residency, and other community documents. For concerns beyond the barangay's jurisdiction, residents may proceed to the appropriate municipal department.
